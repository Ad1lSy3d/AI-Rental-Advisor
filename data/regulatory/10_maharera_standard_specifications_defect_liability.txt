MAHARASHTRA REAL ESTATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY (MAHARERA)
MahaRERA Building Handover, Workmanship Standards and Structural Defect Guidelines
Government of Maharashtra Notification under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016

1. STRUCTURAL DEFECT AND WORKMANSHIP LIABILITY:
Section 14(3) Principles: In case any structural defect or any other defect in workmanship, quality, or provision of services or any other obligations of the promoter/landlord is brought to notice within a period of five years from the date of handing over possession, it shall be the duty of the builder/owner to rectify such defects without further charge, within thirty days.
Key actionable structural defects include:
(a) Structural cracks appearing in reinforced concrete columns, beams, or shear walls.
(b) Severe moisture penetration, efflorescence, and water leakages through building external envelopes, terraces, or plumbing shafts.
(c) Defective electrical wiring leading to persistent tripping, ungrounded outlets, or fire hazards.
(d) Faulty drainage lines causing sewer backflow or offensive odors in living spaces.

2. STATUTORY HABITABILITY VERIFICATION:
Before leasing or occupation of newly constructed premises, the owner shall ensure that:
(a) An authentic Occupancy Certificate (OC) or Completion Certificate (CC) has been issued by the competent municipal planning authority (e.g. BMC, PMC, PCMC).
(b) Permanent connections for potable water supply, electricity distribution, and municipal sewage connections are fully commissioned.
(c) Elevators have received operational safety inspection certificates from the State Electrical Inspectorate.
(d) Common fire safety infrastructure, including wet risers, yard hydrants, and hose reels, has received a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Fire Department.
