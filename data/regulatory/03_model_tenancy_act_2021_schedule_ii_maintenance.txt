MODEL TENANCY ACT, 2021 - SCHEDULE II
Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India
STATUTORY DIVISION OF MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR RESPONSIBILITIES BETWEEN LANDLORD AND TENANT

In the absence of any agreement to the contrary in writing, the responsibilities for maintenance and repairs shall be apportioned between the landlord and the tenant as set out in Part A and Part B of this Schedule.

PART A: RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE LANDLORD
The landlord shall be responsible for carrying out and bearing the expenses of the following structural and major maintenance works:
1. Structural repairs of the building, including foundations, columns, beams, load-bearing walls, and roofs.
2. External whitewashing and painting of the exterior walls, common facades, and compound boundaries.
3. Plumbing maintenance involving the replacement and repair of main water supply pipelines and primary sewage/drainage conduits.
4. Internal and external electrical wiring and maintenance of central electrical distribution switchboards.
5. Maintenance, servicing, and testing of shared elevators and mechanical lifts.
6. Maintenance of emergency fire fighting equipment and common fire sprinkler systems.
7. Structural damp-proofing, waterproofing of roofs and terraces, and repairing exterior facade leaks.
8. External door and window frame replacement necessitated by structural deterioration or dry rot not caused by tenant negligence.

PART B: RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE TENANT
The tenant shall be responsible for carrying out and bearing the expenses of the following routine day-to-day upkeep and repairs:
1. Changing of tap washers, internal faucets, and kitchen sink seals.
2. Routine cleaning and unblocking of kitchen drains, bathroom traps, and water closet waste connections.
3. Maintenance and servicing of internal electrical switches, socket outlets, and fuse replacements.
4. Maintenance and cleaning of geysers, air conditioners, exhaust fans, and water purifiers installed for tenant's private use.
5. Replacement of damaged door locks, latches, cupboard handles, and window glass panes broken during occupancy.
6. Routine painting of interior walls, minor plaster touch-ups, and interior cleaning.
7. Proper sanitation and disposal of domestic waste, kitchen garbage, and debris in municipal waste bins.
8. Ensuring that drainage lines are not clogged with oil, solid food waste, or sanitary hygiene products.

SECTION 16: FAILURE TO CARRY OUT REPAIRS:
(1) If the landlord fails to carry out the repairs specified in Part A after receipt of notice from the tenant within fifteen days, the tenant may carry out such repairs and deduct the cost from the rent payable. Provided that the amount deducted shall not exceed fifty percent of the monthly rent in any single month.
(2) If the tenant fails to carry out the routine repairs specified in Part B after receipt of notice from the landlord, the landlord may cause such repairs to be done and recover the actual documented cost from the tenant's security deposit or as additional rent.
